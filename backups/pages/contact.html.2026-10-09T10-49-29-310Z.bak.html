❓ VISITING GUIDE

Frequently Asked Questions About Visiting

Everything you need to know before walking through our doors.

Q: When is the best time to visit to see live classes?

We recommend morning visits between 9:00 AM and 11:30 AM. During this window, you can observe energetic circle times, phonics lessons, and STEM maker workshops in full session.

Q: Should my child accompany us on the campus tour?

Yes, absolutely! We strongly encourage bringing your child. It allows them to explore our green courtyards and play areas, helping them feel excited and comfortable about their future school.

Q: Is visitor parking available on the campus?

Yes. Crayon Box School features safe, secure visitor parking directly inside our gated 3-acre grounds. Our security guards will guide you upon entry at the main gate.

Q: What documents should we bring for on-the-spot enrollment?

If you wish to initiate enrollment on the same day, please bring: (1) Child’s birth certificate copy, (2) 4 passport photos, (3) Parent address proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID), and (4) Previous grade report card (if applying for Class 1–8).
