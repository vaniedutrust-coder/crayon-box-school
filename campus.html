🛡️ SAFETY AS A SACRED COMMITMENT

Our 5-Pillar Child Protection Shield 💚

In North Delhi, nothing matters more to parents than uncompromising child security. We operate a multi-layered protective envelope ensuring safe transit, hygienic nourishment, and rapid medical response.

🛡️
PILLAR 01

Perimeter & Digital Visitor Shield

High boundary walls with manned 24/7 security stations. All visitors undergo biometric photo-capture, government ID check-in, and instant SMS alerts before entering premises.

  • 60+ Full-HD Night-Vision CCTV Cameras
  • Zero unmonitored gates or boundary entries
  • Pre-authorized digital parent pickup passes
🩺
PILLAR 02

Full-Time Pediatric Infirmary

Dedicated clean medical station staffed by a certified, full-time registered pediatric nurse for immediate first-aid, temperature monitoring, and routine medical attention.

  • Emergency oxygen cylinder & pediatric nebulizer
  • Tie-up with leading multi-specialty hospitals in North Delhi
  • Immediate parent phone notification for any incident
👥
PILLAR 03

100% Police-Verified Personnel

Comprehensive police background checks and psychometric screening for every teacher, administrative staff, driver, conductor, and groundskeeper prior to joining.

  • Active POSH & POCSO Child Safety Committee
  • Female caregiver present on every school floor
  • Zero unsupervised third-party maintenance staff
💧
PILLAR 04

5-Stage RO Water & Pure Dining

Industrial-grade reverse osmosis water purification with copper enrichment stations across every wing, coupled with strict food hygiene audits.

  • Monthly certified lab test reports of drinking water
  • Zero-chemical sanitized dining hall tables
  • Nutritious, vegetarian, zero-junk food policy
🧲
PILLAR 05

Fire Safety & Seismic Readiness

Delhi Fire Service compliant infrastructure with multi-zone smoke detectors, emergency exit stairwells with anti-slip nosing, and bi-monthly evacuation drills.

  • Fire extinguishers inspected & certified monthly
  • Dedicated wide emergency assembly zones on lawn
  • Staff trained in CPR and first-responder protocols

PARENT QUERIES

Frequently Asked Campus Questions 🌿

Common questions regarding physical campus visits, child security protocols, outdoor play during changing seasons, and transport coverage.

Yes, absolutely. We encourage prospective families to observe our authentic learning environment during active class hours. To preserve instructional quiet and child safety, campus walkthroughs are guided by an admissions counselor along designated viewing corridors. Please book a slot in advance using our tour scheduler.

We actively monitor daily AQI and wet-bulb temperatures. On high-heat afternoons or severe winter smog days, outdoor athletic activities transition smoothly to our indoor multi-activity hall and yoga studio. Outdoor PE sessions are scheduled during early morning hours when ambient air temperature and air quality are at their freshest.

Yes. Our foundational years (Nursery, LKG, UKG) occupy an entirely dedicated, gated ground-floor wing with their own private sensory courtyard, low-height washrooms, and separate dismissal gates. This ensures younger children play and transition without feeling overwhelmed by older students.

Parents receive secure credentials for our dedicated transport mobile application. The app displays real-time GPS telemetry, estimated time of arrival (ETA) at your doorstep or society gate, bus speed, and instant notifications when your child boards and disembarks. A dedicated female attendant accompanies students on every single vehicle.

Our on-campus pediatric infirmary is staffed by a qualified full-time nurse. In any event of fever, injury, or discomfort, the student is escorted immediately to the infirmary, vital signs are recorded, first-aid is administered, and parents are contacted directly. For severe emergencies, we have priority tie-ups with leading multi-specialty hospitals within a 10-minute radius in North Delhi.

All drinking water on campus passes through a high-capacity commercial 5-stage reverse osmosis (RO) system followed by UV sterilization and copper enrichment. Water quality and TDS levels are tested daily by our estate manager, and certified water purity laboratory reports are displayed publicly every quarter.
