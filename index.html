HOW CHILDREN LEARN

Four Concrete Teaching Principles in Every Classroom 🌱

We replace passive memorization with active inquiry. Here is what daily learning actually looks like at Crayon Box School:

Phonics & Rich Literacy

In Practice: Multi-sensory letter hunts, rhyme circles, and guided reading corners build natural fluency and vocabulary before Class 1.

Concrete Math Investigations

In Practice: Abacus bead strings, base-10 blocks, and outdoor geometry measurements develop intuitive number sense before abstract formulas.

Experiential STEM Inquiry

In Practice: Hands-on soil and plant experiments in our 3-acre gardens, weather tracking stations, and introductory robotics coding kits.

Character & Confident Voice

In Practice: Daily morning circle reflections, bilingual student presentations, and collaborative theatrical productions nurture empathy and poise.
