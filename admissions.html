What to Bring for Admission

To ensure a seamless enrollment process, please keep self-attested photocopies of the following documents ready when visiting our campus:

  • ✓
    Original & Photocopy of Child’s Birth Certificate Issued by Municipal Corporation or authorized Registrar.
  • ✓
    4 Recent Passport-Size Photographs of Child Along with 2 passport photographs of each parent/guardian.
  • ✓
    Proof of Residence in Delhi Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Electricity/Water utility bill.
  • ✓
    Aadhaar Card of Child & Parents For student state educational tracking records (DISE).
  • ✓
    Previous Year’s Report Card / Marks Sheet Applicable only for Class 2 and above seeking lateral transfer.
  • ✓
    Transfer Certificate (TC) Counter-signed by education department (for Class 2 and above).

Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Here are answers to the questions parents ask most often when visiting our Burari campus.

Kindergarten (Pre-School & Pre-Primary) runs from 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM, structured with healthy snack breaks, sensory play, and circle time. Primary and Middle School (Classes 1 through 8) operate from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, incorporating full academic periods, lab sessions, library hours, and sports.

Yes! We operate safe, GPS-enabled school transport with verified female attendants and speed governors covering Burari, Shastri Park Extension, Sant Nagar, Nathupura, Kamalpur, Timarpur, Civil Lines, Mukherjee Nagar, and GTB Nagar. Pickup points are mapped conveniently to residential gates.

We maintain an intimate teacher-to-student ratio of 1:15 in Kindergarten with co-teachers and dedicated support nannies, and 1:25 in Primary & Middle school. This guarantees that no child is lost in the crowd and every learner receives individualized encouragement.

Crayon Box School follows a rigorous curriculum fully aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and CBSE framework. In early years, we integrate experiential inquiry and hands-on discovery, seamlessly expanding toward middle school analytics and the senior secondary K–12 board horizon.

Child safety is paramount. Our campus features 100% CCTV perimeter coverage, gated biometric entry, security guards, background-verified support staff, clean RO drinking water points, and a full-time nursing health bay.
