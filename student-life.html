PARENT INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions About Student Life

Everything you need to know about after-school clubs, sports coaching, and co-curricular inclusion.

Are co-curricular clubs and sports included in the regular school fee?

Yes, 100%. All core co-curricular clubs, weekly physical education, music, art ateliers, yoga, and house competitions are fully integrated into the school timetable at no extra charge.

How does the school ensure every child gets stage and sports participation?

We practice a strict Zero-Sideline Policy. In our annual productions (Kala Utsav) and sports meet (Urja), 100% of enrolled students participate in costume, performance, or team events—not just a select few.

How are children allocated to the 4 Houses? Can siblings be in the same house?

House allocation occurs upon admission in Class 1. To foster family loyalty and healthy cheer, siblings are automatically placed in the same house so families can celebrate together.

What protective safety gear is mandatory for roller skating and contact sports?

All students entering the roller skating rink must wear helmet, elbow pads, wrist guards, and knee pads provided or verified by school athletic coaches. First-aid personnel and ice packs are stationed ringside.

Can a student switch their chosen club or hobby during the year?

Yes. We encourage multidisciplinary exploration. Students choose their primary atelier each semester, allowing them to study robotics in the monsoon term and clay pottery in the winter term.

How are academic deadlines balanced with intensive event practices?

Event rehearsals are scheduled exclusively during dedicated co-curricular zero periods and sports hours. Core academic instruction hours are strictly protected, ensuring students never fall behind in subjects.
